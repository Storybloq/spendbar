
## Review gate: every premise names its witness (L-006, adopted 2026-08-04)

A review round that STRENGTHENS a contract — a required field, a gate, an invariant — must, in the same round, name the concrete caller that will supply each premise and how that caller can know the value honestly. If no caller can, the contract is weakened to what is witnessable and the ideal moves to the ticket that makes it witnessable. Rationale: T-010 shipped consumed-offset dominance no caller could satisfy; four T-011 designs died manufacturing the premise (ISS-075). This gate would have killed all four in round 3.
