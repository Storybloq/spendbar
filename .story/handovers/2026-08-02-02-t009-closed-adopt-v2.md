## What round 13 actually found (the reason this session mattered)

Taking fresh captures exposed **three checks that had never executed**:

1. The manifest-to-receipt byte binding referenced `manifestBytes`, never declared — every call
   would have thrown `ReferenceError`. It survived eight review rounds because nothing reached it:
   the manifests were stale, so verification refused earlier every time.
2. The verifier's manifest schema had diverged from the producer's on `drainTimedOut`, invisible for
   exactly as long as the committed manifests predated the field.
3. A mutation sweep then found **11 of 13 receipt guards surviving** — nothing had ever observed
   them refuse anything. All 13 are killed now.

The method is reusable and cheap: disable one refusal at a time, re-run the suite, see which
survive. A passing suite says nothing about how many of its guards are alive. Worth running against
any fail-closed check set in this repo.

## Open, and blocked on money — do not start these casually

**ISS-058** (seven reviewed capture-harness defects, one critical: a swallowed write error can
truncate a retained transcript while the digest still authenticates the truncated file) and
**ISS-057** (`--purge` destroys un-receipted captures) are both open and both **batched behind
explicit owner authorization**.

The reason is structural and is the single most important thing to carry forward:
`spikes/mcp/real-client/capture.mjs` is a **capture input**. Its digest is bound into every manifest
and receipt. Editing one character invalidates all four committed captures, the gate refuses with
"capture input(s) changed since the run", and the outcome reverts adopt-v2 → incomplete. There is no
cheap partial fix and no "just the critical one" — any change costs a **four-cell paid recapture**.

So: fix ISS-057 and ISS-058 **together, in one change, with one authorized recapture**. Anything
else spends the same money twice.

**The general lesson:** the capture-input binding is what makes the evidence honest — it is why a
stale capture cannot pass as current — and its cost is that harness bugs found after a capture cost
money to fix, with the cost never falling. Evidence that sits gets more expensive to correct, not
less.
