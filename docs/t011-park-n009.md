## Park reason — AC 1 is blocked on work trapped inside parked T-025

AC 1 requires a real bootstrap publish ("a new generation is published"). Publishing requires a
valid `Provenance`, and `Provenance.ccusageFetchedAt` is a **required scalar**
(`src/snapshot/types.ts:294`). **ISS-077** records that it cannot be honestly populated from the
invocation. So T-011's bootstrap publisher has exactly two options, and both are refused:

- write the invocation instant under a name that means "fetched at" → **knowingly false provenance**;
- omit the field → **fails `assertExactKeys`**, no publish at all.

The fix is the rename to `ccusageInvokedAt` — which is **T-025 item 5**, and T-025 is parked on
ISS-090/ISS-091. A parked ticket does not land its subset automatically. The review last round
confirmed items 2 and 5 are independently landable, so the unblock is cheap — but *making* it land is
a scope decision between tickets, and a planner does not move scope between tickets.

**Cheapest unblock, for the owner:** split T-025 items 2 (`readGeneration`) and 5 (the
`ccusageInvokedAt` rename) into one small landable ticket. That single action unblocks AC 1 here,
gives the pin path a real store-side read (below), and was already confirmed as a sound subset.

## The 9 fixable findings — recorded so a revision does not rediscover them

Two of these are mine and are worth naming plainly, because they are the project's two standing
defect classes committed *in the section where I claimed to be guarding against them*:

1. **I invoked L-006 in §2 and then violated it.** §2 says "read the manifest" — and **T-010 exports
   no manifest-only read API**. Service code would have to duplicate guarded envelope/invariant
   logic, hold a possibly-stale in-memory manifest, or call `classifyStore` (which scans and
   validates the whole store). No concrete caller named, which is precisely the failure L-006 exists
   to catch. **Fix:** a store-owned pin-authorization operation; never reimplement manifest decoding
   under `src/service`.
2. **Defect class (b), in the paragraph where I claimed to avoid it.** I argued "retained is not
   readable" and thought that made me careful. The sharper truth: manifest membership proves an id is
   **referenced for retention** — not that the artifact is **present**, let alone readable.
   `readSnapshot` separately opens, mode-checks, decodes, checksum-validates and invariant-validates
   every referenced generation *because membership proves none of that*. A missing or corrupted
   retained artifact passes my check, receives a **successful pin response**, and is later unreadable
   or has its pin collected — which is ISS-083's exact case, the one I claimed to close. Single-writer
   serialization stops service GC racing the check; it does not turn "referenced" into "present".
   **ISS-083 stays open, and my §2 must be described as a retention-membership subset, not a closure.**
